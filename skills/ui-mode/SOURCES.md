# Acknowledgments

ui-mode is original writing: its wording, tokens, checklists, and ladder are its own. It grew out of years of shared practice in interface design, and these works shaped how its author thinks about the craft. They're recommended reading and watching. No text from them is reproduced here; where an idea or example traces back to one of them, ui-mode restates it in its own words.

- ***Refactoring UI*** by Adam Wathan and Steve Schoger ([refactoringui.com](https://refactoringui.com/)): a book on practical visual design for developers.
- **Kole Jain** ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@kolejain)): videos on product UI, flows, and interaction detail.
- **uxpeak** ([YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/@uxpeak)): videos on decision-focused UX and redesign case studies.

Public principles and standards used by name: Fitts's, Hick's, Jakob's, and Tesler's laws; the goal-gradient, IKEA, reciprocity, loss-aversion, and anchoring effects; WCAG 2.2; Apple's Human Interface Guidelines and Material Design target sizes; and the 0.1 s / 1 s / 10 s response-time limits.

ui-mode is not affiliated with or endorsed by any of the people or works above. Product names appear only as examples of shipped patterns.
