# Borrow

Take structure from products that already work, and study what surprises you.

- Treat expected layouts as a feature. Nav at the top or side, content in reading order, the main action obvious. Save novelty for the content.
- Assemble wireframes from proven sections. Take section structures from several real products, keep only their skeletons, and drop their styling; identity comes later (**Soul**).
- Reference shipped products. Mockup galleries and concept shots skip states, data, and edge cases. Study products in daily use (Linear, Stripe, Figma, Notion) and live sites.
- Compare at least three shipped flows. For onboarding, a paywall, checkout, or filters, look at three real implementations before designing, through a screen-library tool if one is connected.
- Hunt for the surprising decision. In a screen you admire, note each value you wouldn't have chosen: tight heading leading, negative tracking, a two-layer shadow, a 13 px label. Those are the lessons.
- Trace to learn. Recreate an admired screen exactly, value for value, without improving it. The reconstruction teaches what looking can't.
- Name your references. Record which product each region's structure came from (build step 5).

**Check:** Can you name the real product behind each major pattern?
