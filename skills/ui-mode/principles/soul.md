# Soul

Choose a personality, and make it belong to the user's world.

- Set the personality early and hold it everywhere. It has four dials:
  - Typeface: serif reads classic or authoritative, rounded sans friendly, neutral sans plain and modern, mono technical.
  - Color: cool blues read calm and trustworthy, saturated warm hues energetic, deep tones with black or gold premium.
  - Radius: small or none reads precise and serious; large reads soft and playful.
  - Voice: from formal to conversational.

  Values: `references/type.md` (Fonts), `references/layout.md` (Radius).
- Look like you know the user's world. A tool for bakeries should feel like it has seen a bakery: imagery from that world, the words its people use, examples built on their kind of data.
- Layout and identity are separate layers. The same wireframe can carry very different brands, so choose structure (**Borrow**) and identity (here) separately.
- Keep modern UI from turning sterile with context imagery and a little quirk (a 404 page, an empty-state illustration). That is what makes a product memorable.
- Match effects to the genre and the goal. AI products carry glow and gradients well; conversion pages and data tools stay restrained.

**Check:** Swap in a competitor's logo. Would anything else need to change? If not, the UI has no soul.

**Values:** `references/landing.md` (Identity).
