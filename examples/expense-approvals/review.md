# ui-mode review: Acme Spend approvals dashboard

**Goal.** Critique the screen a team lead at Pinecrest Outdoor Co. uses to approve reimbursements: find what they'd trip on and what looks undecided.
**Surface.** App (SaaS dashboard) carrying a process: each approval is a **commit** that moves money (`references/dashboard.md`, `references/processes.md`).
**Smallest UI that meets the goal.** A queue of pending requests; each opens a review panel with the receipt, the policy check, and the history, one primary "Approve $amount", and a quieter "Return with a reason".

## Verdict

A generated-template approvals dashboard whose biggest problem is that the lead approves money blind: no receipt, policy limit, or history is in view, Reject wears the brand's primary color at equal weight to Approve, and the whole queue breaks at 390 px.

- **Approvals, 1440×900 light: Stock.** Defaults throughout, one emphasis level (19 solid buttons on the first screen), filters pushed into dropdowns. Finding 1 lifts it to Systematic: one primary per view, labeled with its outcome ("Approve $2,310.00"); findings 4, 6, and 14 complete the rung.
- **Approvals, 390×844 light: Stock.** It's the desktop layout clipped at the right edge. Finding 3 lifts it.
